# Media Mingles design contract

- Canvas `#080808`; raised surface `#121212`; primary text `#F2EFE8`; secondary text `#9B978E`; rules use off-white at 14% opacity; accent `#FF5A45`.
- Geist is the main face. Oswald is reserved for labels, numbers and selected italic display words.
- Use a 12-column desktop, 6-column tablet and 4-column mobile grid. Gutters are 40–48px, 24–32px and 18–20px.
- Media corners are 8–12px. Body copy is at least 16px and targets are at least 44px.
- Native scrolling only. Content remains complete without JavaScript.
- Desktop gets full reveals and light parallax; tablet gets shorter reveals; mobile uses normal flow. Reduced motion removes pinning, scrubbing, masks, ticker motion and parallax.
- Only the showreel may pin, capped at 150vh. Every page ends with `Request free audit`.
